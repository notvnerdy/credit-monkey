Understand Late Payments

Late payments can have a major impact on your credit score. Learn how they work and how to improve your credit profile.

Free Consultation

What Are Late Payments?

Late payments occur when a payment is not made by the due date. Once a payment is at least 30 days late, it can be reported to the credit bureaus and impact your credit score.

How Late Payments Affect Your Credit

Payment history is one of the most important factors in credit scoring. A single late payment can significantly lower your score, and repeated late payments can have a lasting effect.

How Credit Monkey Can Help

We review your credit reports for inaccurate or unverifiable late payments and dispute them when appropriate. Our process is transparent, compliant, and focused on helping you improve your credit profile.

Best Practices Moving Forward

  • Set up payment reminders or automatic payments.
  • Prioritize on-time payments to rebuild history.
  • Monitor your credit reports regularly.
