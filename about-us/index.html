LEGAL COMPLIANCE & TRANSPARENCY

Our Legal & Compliance Commitments

Credit repair is a YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) service. We take our legal obligations seriously and go beyond minimum compliance requirements.

CROA Compliance

The Credit Repair Organizations Act (15 U.S.C. § 1679) requires us to provide you with a written disclosure of your rights before any contract is signed. Credit Monkey provides this Consumer Rights Notice with every new client agreement.

  • No fees charged before services are fully rendered
  • Mandatory 3-business-day cancellation right
  • Written contract with all services clearly described
  • Consumer Rights Disclosure provided before signing

FCRA & Consumer Rights

Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681), every consumer has the right to dispute inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, or unverifiable information on their credit report — at no charge. Credit Monkey's dispute process is built on these legal rights.

  • Right to dispute inaccurate information for free
  • Bureaus must investigate within 30–45 days
  • Outdated negative items must be removed after 7 years
  • Unverifiable items must be deleted from your report

Important Disclosures

Credit Monkey cannot guarantee the removal of accurate, current, and verifiable negative information from your credit report. No credit repair company legally can. Individual results vary based on the type and complexity of items on your report, the credit bureaus' investigation outcomes, and other factors outside our control.

You have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost. Credit Monkey provides a professional service to assist and streamline that process. Read our full disclosures.
