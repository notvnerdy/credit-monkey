Free Credit Repair Tool

Use this checklist to organize credit repair work before a consultation or before sending your own disputes.

  • Pull all three credit reports
  • Save account statements and notices
  • List inaccurate dates, balances, owners, or statuses
  • Separate identity theft concerns from ordinary disputes
  • Track dispute dates and bureau responses
  • Keep copies of every letter and upload
  • Pair disputes with utilization and payment-history work

How to Use This Tool

Use it for education and planning. It does not replace a full three-bureau credit report review or legal advice.

  • Review all three bureaus
  • Document the facts
  • Track dates and responses
  • Ask for help when files are complex
