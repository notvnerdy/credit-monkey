# Credit Monkey Off-Site SEO Action Plan

## Priority 1: Google Business Profile

- Verify or reclaim the official Credit Monkey profile.
- Use one exact business name, phone number, website URL, and service-area description everywhere.
- Add services for credit repair, credit restoration, credit consultation, collections review, late payment review, identity theft credit support, and credit score improvement where available.
- Add branded photos, team or office photos if available, and service graphics that match the website.
- Publish one privacy-safe credit education post per week.
- Request honest customer reviews after meaningful service milestones. Never request reviews that disclose private credit details.
- Respond to every review with neutral, privacy-safe language.

## Priority 2: Citation Cleanup

- Build one NAP standard: Credit Monkey Credit Repair Services, verified phone number, and `https://creditmonkey.com/`.
- Audit existing listings for inconsistent names, old URLs, tracking numbers, and mismatched service areas.
- Submit or update core listings: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, BBB if eligible, Chamber of Commerce profiles, local California directories, and relevant finance/legal-adjacent directories.
- Track citation URL, login owner, status, date updated, and consistency notes.

## Priority 3: Link Acquisition

- Build partnerships with mortgage brokers, real estate agents, auto finance professionals, personal finance educators, California consumer resource sites, and local business organizations.
- Pitch educational assets already published on the site: credit utilization calculator, dispute timeline calculator, mortgage credit repair guide, auto loan credit repair guide, and local California pages.
- Avoid paid link schemes, private blog networks, mass guest posting, or anchor-text manipulation.

## Priority 4: Digital PR Topics

- California credit readiness before mortgage preapproval.
- How medical collections reporting changes affect consumers.
- Credit repair after divorce or identity theft.
- Auto loan approval preparation after repossession.
- Consumer documentation checklist for credit report disputes.

## Priority 5: Measurement

- Track rankings weekly for brand, national commercial, California, Los Angeles, and issue-based terms.
- Track Google Search Console impressions by page cluster.
- Track assisted conversions from comparison pages and tools.
- Refresh underperforming pages every 60-90 days with better FAQs, examples, and internal links.
