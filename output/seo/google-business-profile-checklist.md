# Google Business Profile Checklist

- Confirm official business name and avoid keyword stuffing.
- Set the canonical website to `https://creditmonkey.com/`.
- Use the same public phone number shown on citations and the website.
- Choose the most accurate available primary category.
- Add service descriptions using compliance-safe language.
- Add service areas for California and major cities where the business actively serves clients.
- Upload branded logo, cover image, and real team or office photos if available.
- Add weekly posts around credit education, not guaranteed outcomes.
- Add FAQ-style Q&A entries with consumer-rights focused answers.
- Ask customers for honest reviews after service milestones.
- Reply to reviews without exposing private financial or credit details.
- Track calls, direction requests, website clicks, and profile searches monthly.
